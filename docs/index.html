About / Attributions

An unofficial, non-commercial fan tool for tracking your MHGU equipment collection.

Development: Built by the author with substantial help from Claude Code, Anthropic's AI coding tool. Most of the code, the data-extraction scripts and this page were written with AI assistance, reviewed and directed by the author.

Armor data: Extracted from the game's own files — a personally-owned cartridge, no game files redistributed. Extraction made possible by Atmosphère, hekate, Lockpick_RCM and nxdumptool, and by the Ryujinx emulator's RomFS extractor.

Weapon & Palico data: Kiranico

Crafting materials (weapons & Palico) and English item, skill and equipment names: MHGUDB-iOS (MIT) & gatheringhallstudios community database. The game files store names in Japanese only, so the English naming throughout this tool comes from their work.

Equipment icons: monsterhunterwiki.org — licensed under CC BY-SA 4.0; adaptations shared under the same licence.

Monster icons (theme picker): monsterhunter.fandom.com — licensed under CC BY-SA 3.0.

Menu textures: Edited from the developer's in-game screenshots.

Monster Hunter content is owned by Capcom. This tool is not affiliated with or endorsed by Capcom.

Other MHGU Apps

Companion fan tools by the same author. Each opens in a new tab.

  • Quest Randomizer Rolls random quests when you can't decide what to hunt.
  • Collection Tracker you are here Tracks which weapons, armor and Palico gear you own, and what it costs to finish them.
  • Equipment Box Mirrors the in-game box — 2000 hunter slots and 1000 Palico — so you can plan where things sit.
  • Bingo Builds a bingo card of hunting goals, with a shareable seed so a group can race the same board.
  • Hunting Log Keeps a running record of your hunts — what you were after, what you wore, who came along.
  • Zenny Gauntlet A scored challenge run — fail a hunt or get carted and you lose that weapon/style combo for the rest of the run.
  • Set Builder Assemble a full set by hand and see exactly which skills it activates.
  • Monster Info Hitzone charts for every monster — where to hit and with what.

Settings

Storage
Save in this browser Keeps your collection here so it loads automatically. Use Save to a file for backups or another device.
Equipment Box
Mark pieces owned from the Equipment Box Anything in your box counts as owned here. Turn this off if you track across more than one save file, so a single box can't narrow your collection.
Interaction
Click to mark owned and increase level Clicking an open piece again marks it owned, then raises its level each click.
Ctrl + Click to remove Un-owns a piece of equipment.
Alt + Click to fully upgrade Marks a piece owned at its max level in one click.
Display
Include DUMMY (MHXX) Weapons Shows the seven MHXX-only weapons left behind in MHGU's data. They can't be obtained in-game.
Theme
